Know which desk owns the request

Cutler Bay is a town, while several large household services are delivered by Miami-Dade County. This guide separates the two so residents can get to the right place first.

The Town handles

Permits, roads, drainage & code

Town Public Works maintains town infrastructure such as streets, sidewalks, storm drains, canals, and right-of-way landscaping. Building & Code Services handles permitting, inspections, and code concerns.

The County often handles

Household trash & recycling

For many homes, Miami-Dade Solid Waste provides garbage twice each week and recycling every other week. HOA communities may have a different provider. Confirm your address before setting items out.
