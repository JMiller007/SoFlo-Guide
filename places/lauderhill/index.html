GOOD TO KNOW

AutoPay needs a fresh setup

Lauderhill says its new utility payment system launched April 6, 2026. Previous online accounts and AutoPay enrollments did not carry over; register again to keep online payments or automatic drafts active.

LOCAL DETAIL

Urgent water problem after hours?

The Utilities Department lists 954-730-2972 for after-hours emergencies. The Utilities Facility is at 2101 NW 49th Avenue; utility billing is handled at City Hall, 5581 W. Oakland Park Blvd.
