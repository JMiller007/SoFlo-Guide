SOUTH MIAMI · THE CITY OF PLEASANT LIVING

Follow the day.
Follow the right route.

South Miami’s civic life centers on Sunset Drive, with several City departments based at the Sylva Martin Building. City solid-waste crews and County recycling run on different calendars. This guide keeps the weekly routes, permit counter and proper disposal rules together.

A useful counter cutoff

Permit counter closes before City Hall does.

South Miami City Hall is open weekdays, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. The Building Division permit counter is open 8 a.m.–3 p.m. If you need a next-day inspection, call before 3:45 p.m. and have your permit number ready.

CITY · BULK

Weekly route, size limits apply

The Solid Waste Division provides weekly scheduled bulk service up to one-half truckload (12.5 cubic yards) without charge. Keep piles in front of your residence and clear of drains, meters and obstructions.

COUNTY · RECYCLING

Wednesday County service

Miami-Dade County recycling is collected on Wednesdays. The County supplies and replaces damaged recycling containers; contact 311 for cart issues.

Recycling guidance ↗

Appliances & white goods

Washers, dryers, refrigerators and other large appliances may have special handling. The City asks residents to contact Public Works at 305-663-6350 before placing white goods out.

Construction debris & chemicals

Construction/remodel debris, tires, vehicle parts, paint and hazardous items are not regular City curbside pickup. Use Miami-Dade’s disposal facilities and household chemical collection guidance.

Keep bulk piles on your own frontage. Don’t share piles, cover drains or place items beside water-meter boxes. Construction debris remains the property owner or contractor’s disposal responsibility.
