Manage a City utility bill

LOCAL NETWORK · TWO WATER SOURCES

Why service can vary by area

The City provides water and wastewater service across Hialeah and also serves some nearby unincorporated areas. Hialeah purchases most drinking water from Miami-Dade County, while its jointly owned reverse-osmosis plant can produce up to 7.5 million gallons daily for the northwest part of the City. The southeast is served from Miami-Dade’s John E. Preston plant.

City wastewater infrastructure includes 101 lift-station service areas and roughly 338 miles of gravity sewer lines. For outages and advisories, check the City’s active notice before relying on a general map.

GARBAGE · TWICE WEEKLY

Check the collection area first

Residential garbage is collected twice weekly. Depending on the assigned area, pickup is Monday/Thursday or Tuesday/Friday. Use the City collection map for your actual days. Place the brown 96-gallon City cart out the night before or before 6 a.m.; keep it at least five feet from vehicles, mailboxes and other obstacles, with the lid fully closed.

RECYCLING · BULK · YARD WASTE

Use the map, not a citywide guess

Residential recycling is scheduled twice a month; bulky and yard waste are collected once a month. Routes vary by area. Do not mix household garbage with yard or bulk debris. Missed collection, damaged cart, disability side-door service and prohibited items are handled through the Public Works Solid Waste Division.

BUILD · RENOVATE · INSPECT

Community Development & Building

Most construction, repair, demolition, relocation or occupancy changes require a permit before work begins. Hialeah’s Building Department reviews applications for Florida Building Code compliance; permit types, fees and inspection scheduling are on the City portal.

Building questions: 305-883-5825. City Hall: 501 Palm Avenue. The City’s Citizen Self-Service portal is used for permits issued through Tyler.

PLANNING · BUSINESS · LAND USE

Ask zoning before signing or building

Planning and Zoning is on the second floor of City Hall. Check that a proposed business use, building or renovation fits City zoning before leasing a space or starting work. Business Tax, code enforcement and planning are within Community Development but may have separate applications.

RECORDS · COMMISSION · ELECTIONS

City Clerk, third floor

The Hialeah City Clerk is corporate secretary to the City Commission and local supervisor of elections. The Clerk’s Office handles official records, Commission documents and records requests. In-person public records requests go to the third floor at 501 Palm Avenue.

Reserve a park or visit Milander
