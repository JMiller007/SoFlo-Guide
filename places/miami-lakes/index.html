Know who does what

County-run home services

Miami Lakes’ Town Guide routes residents to Miami-Dade County for household waste, water and sewer, recycling, fire rescue and mosquito control. Most residential properties receive automated waste pickup twice a week; use the address lookup for your actual collection days. Town FAQ says eligible residents may schedule two bulky-waste pickups per year by calling 311.
