GOOD TO KNOW

HPS is a separate city utility

Homestead Public Services handles municipal electric, water, and sanitation service. For account, billing, or shutoff questions call 305-224-4800; trash-pickup questions go to 305-224-4860.

LOCAL DETAIL

Permit help can be face-to-face

Homestead advertises a homeowner and small-business concierge plus a monthly walkthrough partnership with Miami-Dade County for select permit matters. The city currently lists the walkthrough on the fourth Thursday, 8 a.m.–noon; check the page for requirements before attending.
