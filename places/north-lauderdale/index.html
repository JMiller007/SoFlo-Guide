North Lauderdale

North Lauderdale’s most-used resident tasks start at two desks: Utility Billing for water accounts and the City’s Citizen Self Service portal for development records. This guide lays out what you can do online and when to visit City Hall.

A quick route through City Hall

Start with the task

North Lauderdale’s utility account tools and permit self-service are online. For a visit, note that City Hall and cashier hours are not the same.

Most used resident desk

Water & utility billing

View a bill, make a one-time payment, or register to manage accounts and payment reminders. Quick Pay requires your account number; supported options include card and e-check.
