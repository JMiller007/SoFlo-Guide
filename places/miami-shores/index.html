Miami Shores Village · a canopy-minded community

The village
under the trees.

Miami Shores has a distinctly local service network: Village Public Works looks after streets, trees, drainage, parks and household sanitation. Water and sewer questions go to Miami-Dade County, and many homes still use septic systems.

Start with the Village

Most day-to-day neighborhood concerns can be routed through Miami Shores Village. Use MyMSV for online service requests; Public Works has a different desk from Building and Planning.

Street, tree, drain or bulk item

Public Works

Public Works maintains Village streets and signs, public trees, storm drains, drainage pumps, six Village parks, and sanitation service. Contact them about a clogged drain, a deceased animal in the roadway, bulk or sanitation routing, or right-of-way concerns.

Counter: Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Main line: 305-795-2210.

Permit, inspection or business

Building & Planning

The Building Department handles local permits and inspections; Planning and Zoning reviews land use. The Village's online Citizen Self-Service portal supports applications and status searches. A driveway approach or work in the public right-of-way may also require Public Works approval.

Building: 305-795-2204 · Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Planning & Zoning: 305-762-4864.

Parks & Recreation

Know the handoff

The Village is not the water utility. For water and wastewater, Miami-Dade County or North Miami is the service provider depending on location and connection. Some homes rely on septic systems.

Septic-to-sewer is a live infrastructure topic.

Miami Shores does not operate a sanitary sewer utility. Some neighborhoods have County or North Miami connections; many properties remain on septic. Ask the utility serving your exact address before planning work or troubleshooting a sewer issue.
