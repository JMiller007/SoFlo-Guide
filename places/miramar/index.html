Miramar coordinates resident services through City departments and service partners. The details below help separate the bill, pickup and permit pathways.

01 / Utilities · eBill

Pay or manage your City utility bill

Online bill payment requires the Customer ID number printed on your bill. The City offers eBill and AutoPay sign-up; credit/debit transactions carry a 2.95% processing fee. The residential sanitation fee is included on the monthly water/utility bill. For bill help, call 954-602-HELP (4357).

02 / Waste Pro · route by address

Garbage, recycling and bulk

Green garbage carts are collected twice weekly, blue recycling carts once weekly, and bulk once monthly. Collection days and bulk routes depend on address. The City contracts Waste Pro for collection; call Waste Pro at 954-967-4200 about a missed pickup or damaged cart. Alert Miramar can send route notifications by text, phone or email.

03 / Hazardous household materials

Use the monthly drop-off event

Miramar offers a free household hazardous-material drop-off on the first Saturday of each month, 8 a.m.–2 p.m., at 17200 Pines Boulevard. Bring a driver’s license and proof of Miramar residency; remain in your vehicle and follow the event instructions. Check the City’s page for accepted materials and schedule changes before going.

04 / Building · permits & inspections

Submit online, then follow the review

Miramar’s permit portal accepts applications 24/7 and supports payments, status checks and inspection scheduling. The Building counter provides over-the-counter assistance Monday–Thursday, 7 a.m.–5 p.m. Permit type and plan requirements vary by work; check the City’s permit guidance before beginning construction.

05 / Reports · records · requests

Use the Customer Service hub

From the City’s online services, residents can submit public-records requests, code complaints and police-report requests, check code-case status, and reach other service forms. For non-emergency questions, email customerservice@miramarfl.gov or call 954-602-HELP.

06 / Business · fees & applications

Know which tasks are online

Business Tax Receipt fees can be paid online, but the City says applications cannot be submitted online and must be completed in person. Credit/debit payments listed on the City payment page carry a processing fee. Confirm required forms and office hours with Customer Service first.

City payment and application notes ↗
Urgent water break or sewer backup?

Miramar Customer Service says urgent requests such as water breaks and sewer backups are accepted 24/7. Call the City Help line for urgent routing; do not wait for an online form.
