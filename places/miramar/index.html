GOOD TO KNOW

Need urgent water or sewer help?

Miramar lists 24/7 response for urgent water breaks and sewer backups. Call Customer Service at 954-602-HELP (4357). For non-urgent matters, use the online service portal.

LOCAL DETAIL

First Saturday hazardous-waste drop-off

The city’s solid-waste page lists a free monthly drop-off at 17200 Pines Blvd., 8 a.m.–2 p.m. Bring photo ID and proof of residency; confirm the next date before going.
