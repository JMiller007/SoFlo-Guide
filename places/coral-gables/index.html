Daily life, routed clearly

Coral Gables combines a commission-manager government with several highly local services. Start by matching the request to the city team that actually handles it.

A strong local route

Waste pickup, permits, and moving around town.

Single-family city collection includes backyard garbage twice a week, backyard recycling once a week, and curbside trash once a week. Multi-family and commercial collection is handled by Waste Management; use the address-based city schedule to confirm your pickup.
