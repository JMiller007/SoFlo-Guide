A useful place to begin

The city lists regular residential garbage twice a week and recycling once a week. Bulk is weekly for households with curbside garbage collection; hazardous waste is not accepted with regular curbside trash.

This guide summarizes public information and helps route you to the right service. Follow the city’s source for current requirements and address-specific details.

Waste & utilities

Garbage, recycling & bulk

Margate’s residential curbside garbage is collected twice weekly, recycling once weekly, and bulk weekly for households receiving curbside garbage collection.

GOOD TO KNOW

Don’t put household chemicals at the curb

Margate’s waste page says household hazardous waste is not collected curbside and should not be mixed with regular garbage. Check the city and county options before transporting materials.

LOCAL DETAIL

Building has a separate service address

City Hall is at 5790 Margate Boulevard. The Building Department lists 901 NW 66th Avenue, with weekday service hours and online ProjectDox permitting. Confirm before making an in-person trip.
