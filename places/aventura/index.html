Aventura · The City of Excellence

Life, in motion.

Aventura’s civic experience is shaped by two practical ideas: services are often delivered through specialist providers, and city mobility is unusually easy to use without a car. Here’s the guide to getting around, reporting an issue and starting city business.

Aventura Express · free & open to the public

Three routes, one useful connection.

Hourly buses connect residential Aventura to key destinations and the Aventura Mall transit terminal, where riders can transfer to Miami-Dade or Broward Transit.

Purple · North

Northern Aventura route. Check live vehicle location for the nearest stop.

Green · Central

Central Aventura route with access to the mall terminal and local destinations.

Yellow · South

Southern Aventura route, linking the neighborhood to city destinations.

MONDAY–FRIDAY · 6:45 A.M.–7:45 P.M.

All three routes run hourly. No service from 12:45–1:45 p.m. daily. No Sunday service or service on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year’s Day.

SATURDAY · 8:45 A.M.–9:45 P.M.

All buses are ADA accessible. Track live buses and estimated arrivals through the city’s Ride Freebee tracking page. Shuttle line: 305-932-1BUS (1287).

How Aventura delivers services

The City says many public services are provided by contracted specialists. That means residents often need to contact the service department or provider assigned to the issue. Use the City’s service directory to distinguish municipal requests from services managed by Miami-Dade County.
