Doral

Doral 311 routes non-emergency requests and lets residents track them. Some daily services cross jurisdiction lines: Miami-Dade County provides water and sewer while the city manages public stormwater infrastructure.

Trash & recycling: know your hauler

Doral uses several private haulers. The city franchises and monitors operators; your collection schedule depends on your service provider. Public Works can answer franchise questions.

Water, sewer & stormwater

Miami-Dade County WASD supplies water and sewer. Doral maintains public stormwater infrastructure; the County bills the city’s stormwater charge on its behalf.

GOOD TO KNOW

Water, sewer, and storm drains are not the same desk

Contact Miami-Dade Water and Sewer at 305-665-7477 for water or sanitary-sewer service problems. Contact Doral Public Works at 305-593-6740 for city stormwater infrastructure or street concerns.

LOCAL DETAIL

311 is for non-emergencies

Doral 311 is designed for routable service requests such as potholes, signs, trolley requests, and code concerns. The city says it is not an emergency channel; call 911 for emergencies.
