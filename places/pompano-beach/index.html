Explore this service ↗Utility billing

Start or manage water service

New residents can set up water and trash service through Utility Billing at City Hall Room 119 or call 954-786-4637.

Explore this service ↗Solid waste

Garbage & collection routes

Single-family homes, duplexes, and triplexes receive garbage collection twice weekly. Exact days depend on the service area; use city-provided gray carts.

Explore this service ↗Online services

Permits, recreation & records

City e-services include permit and project research, plan review, inspection scheduling, utility billing, RecTrac, records requests, and service reports.

GOOD TO KNOW

Collection schedules vary by address

Pompano’s sanitation page has the route details and holiday exceptions. Environmental Services can help at 954-786-4030. Check the official schedule before setting items out.

LOCAL DETAIL

Downtown City Hall is changing

The city has approved a new City Hall design and announced a new downtown facility project. The current directory lists 100 W. Atlantic Blvd.; verify the visit location before heading in.
