GOOD TO KNOW

Check who provides your waste service

Miami Gardens notes that qualifying households served by Miami-Dade County residential waste can receive two bulky-waste pickups, up to 25 cubic yards each. This is a county service; confirm your account and current rules.

LOCAL DETAIL

Community and education programs

Resident Services includes CityCares and a City University Partnership with Miami Dade College, Florida Memorial University, and St. Thomas University.
