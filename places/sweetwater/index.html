Ride the Sweetwater trolley

A city-operated connection to neighborhood destinations, FIU and major shopping. Check the posted tracker and schedule before setting out.

Public transit · No fare

One route.
Many daily stops.

The trolley runs roughly every 90 minutes. Stops include the Sweetwater Municipal Complex, FIU, International Mall and Dolphin Mall. The route begins at Maintenance & Transit, 1701 NW 110th Avenue.

Who owns the next step?

Sweetwater’s online guides separate County-operated utilities and waste from City-maintained drainage, local streets and permits.

Water & sewer · County

Miami-Dade WASD

County Water and Sewer maintains the water and sanitary sewer system. Call 305-665-7477 for account and service issues. City engineering may still require a right-of-way permit for new connections.

City stormwater FAQ ↗
Garbage & recycling · County

Solid Waste Management

Green-cart garbage is collected twice weekly on assigned routes; Sweetwater lists Tuesday and Friday. Blue-cart recycling is every other Wednesday. Place carts out by 7 a.m. Use 311 for a missed pickup or cart.

Collection, bulky waste & recycling ↗
Engineering · City

Roads, drainage & right-of-way

The City maintains public roads and sidewalks under its jurisdiction and operates public stormwater infrastructure. For a damaged city sidewalk or drainage concern, call Engineering at 305-455-6585.

Engineering Division ↗
Scheduled service · County

Bulky waste appointments

Weekly bulky sweeps have ended. Each household may schedule two pickups per calendar year, up to 25 cubic yards each. Schedule before putting material at the curb by calling 311 or using County Solid Waste online.

County bulky pickup rules ↗
Permits · City

Building & zoning

For building and zoning permit or inspection status, start with the City department. Sweetwater lists 305-485-4526 for building and zoning questions. Public Works permit submissions use a different Engineering route.

Administration · City

Reach City Hall

City Hall is at 500 SW 109 Avenue, Sweetwater, FL 33174. The main city contact number is 305-221-0411. Department hours may differ; confirm the specific counter before visiting.
