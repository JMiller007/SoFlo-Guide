GOOD TO KNOW

Town Hall is open four days a week

Davie Town Hall is listed at 8800 SW 36th Street, Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Confirm the correct building and department before an in-person visit.

LOCAL DETAIL

A newer permit submission route

Starting January 12, 2026, building and permit service applications use the town’s Online Application Submittal (OAS) system. eSuite remains available for public searches and inspection information.
