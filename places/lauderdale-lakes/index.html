Lauderdale Lakes · A community guide

Neighbors first.

Some daily services come from City Hall, others from Broward County or Waste Management. This guide separates the desks so you can get from “what do I need?” to the right office on the first try.

A simple service map

Start with the agency that owns the service.

The city’s phone directory identifies outside utilities and its contracted solid-waste provider. If you’re unsure which side of the counter to use, call City Hall and ask for routing before making a trip.

Lane 01 · City

Permits, records & City matters

Development Services uses OpenGov for online permitting and applications. The permitting counter is at 3521 NW 43rd Avenue inside the Municipal Complex. City Hall administration: 4300 NW 36th Street.

Lane 02 · County

Water account & utilities

The city directs water account questions to Broward County Environmental Services. Call 954-831-3250 for account assistance. For electrical outages or to open an FPL account, call FPL at 954-797-5000.

Outside utility contacts ↗
Lane 03 · Service partner

Garbage, recycling & bulk

Waste Management provides city-franchised waste service. Collection days vary by neighborhood: Eastgate/Northgate Monday, Oriole Estates Tuesday, Oakland Estates/Westgate Wednesday; condominiums have a separate route.

Routes & pickup guidance ↗
Need to report dumping?Public Works environmental-crime line: 954-535-2815. City Public Works lists weekday hours of 8 a.m.–5 p.m., excluding holidays.
