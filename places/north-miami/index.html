Explore this service ↗Utilities

Open a utility account

North Miami asks residents to confirm the property is in the City’s utility service area. Applications are completed in person during weekday business hours with supporting documents.

Explore this service ↗Customer service

Pay a bill in person

The Central Cashier is on the first floor at City Hall. The drop box is at the south entrance; include your bill slip and account number.

GOOD TO KNOW

Confirm the utility service area first

North Miami’s account-opening instructions ask customers to use the County Property Appraiser’s service-area information before applying. If the City serves your property, Utility Billing is 305-895-9880.

LOCAL DETAIL

City Hall is the civic hub

City Hall is at 776 NE 125 Street. It houses City Council Chambers and multiple city departments; check the department directory for floor, appointment, and counter details.
