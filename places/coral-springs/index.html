GOOD TO KNOW

Water service is district-based

The City is one of four water districts in Coral Springs. If the City is your provider, Water Billing is 954-344-1825 or water@coralsprings.gov. Other areas are served by separate districts.

LOCAL DETAIL

Visit the One Stop Shop

Located on the first floor of City Hall. Published hours: Mon–Thu 8 a.m.–4 p.m. and Fri 8 a.m.–1 p.m. Customer Care Call Center: 954-344-1025.
