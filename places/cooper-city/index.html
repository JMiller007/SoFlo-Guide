Cooper City keeps useful services close to the resident: one customer-service number for water and sewer bills, online tools for permits and issues, and City Clerk passport appointments. Here’s the quickest route through the basics.

The community desk

What do you need to get done?

Cooper City puts online service links right on its home page. Use these direct paths for the most common resident tasks.

01 · PayWater & sewer bill

Customer service: 954-434-4300, option 1. Finance manages account billing and payment processing. Check the official payment page for current online methods and any fees.

02 · ReportTextMyGov or report online

Cooper City accepts text keywords such as REPORT, POTHOLE, MISSED PICKUP, UTILITIES and ORDINANCE VIOLATION to help route a neighborhood concern.
