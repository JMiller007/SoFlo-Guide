THREE DESKS · ONE ADDRESS

Choose the right route first.

01 / CITY HALLPermits & property projects

Building permits and inspections are handled by CAP Government. Apply through Citizenserve; next-day inspection requests have a 3 p.m. cutoff. Call 954-989-2688, option 9, extension 202 or 210.

02 / COUNTY PROVIDERWater, power & property tax

Water accounts go through Broward County Water & Wastewater Services. FPL handles electric accounts and outages. The County Property Appraiser handles property tax questions and records.

Public Works oversees solid waste and bulk collection, city streets, parks, drainage and stormwater. Use the live 2026–27 schedule on the City page for zone-specific collection; provider notices have changed over time.
