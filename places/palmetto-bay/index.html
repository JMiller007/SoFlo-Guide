At the Village

Village Hall is open weekdays 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. Building & Permitting has a different counter schedule and closes for lunch.

Permits & property projects

The Building & Permitting Division handles building applications, permit instructions and code requirements. Its listed weekday window is 7:30 a.m.–4 p.m., with a noon–1 p.m. lunch closure.

  • Permit help: 305-259-1250
  • Appointments and forms are available through Village Services.
  • Check permit status and inspection steps before scheduling work.

County services residents use every week

Miami-Dade County provides water and sewer, garbage and trash pickup, recycling, fire services and library services in Palmetto Bay. The Village does not manage utility service. FPL handles electric power.

Collection, explained

Garbage, bulky trash, recycling and electronics recycling are delivered by Miami-Dade Solid Waste for properties in the Village. The County sets the route and answers missed-pickup questions.

For schedule or bulky-pickup help, use County 311 or 305-468-5900. Collection days and rules can vary by address and material.
