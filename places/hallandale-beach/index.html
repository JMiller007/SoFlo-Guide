Hallandale Beach · Broward County

City by the sea.
Services by the day.

Hallandale Beach has a citywide weekly trash rhythm, but the pickup day depends on your quadrant. City Hall also keeps distinct hours for public counters, building intake and utility payments. Here’s the practical version.

A pocket calendar for the curb

Your quadrant sets collection day.

Put your green city-issued household trash cart and eligible debris at the swale by 7 a.m. on the scheduled service day. Use the official map to confirm which quadrant contains your address.
