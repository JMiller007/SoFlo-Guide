Water billing changed hands.

Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer now handles Opa-locka water utility billing and collections. The County customer center is at the Municipal Complex, 780 Fisherman Street, Suite 130. County bill pay by phone: 800-510-0880. For leaks or main breaks: 786-471-4025.

A landmark brought back to life

The City’s adopted FY 2026 budget reports the restoration complete. The landmark reopened for public gatherings in 2025; the City later sought an operator for the historic venue.

Historic City Hall

Opa-locka is known for a distinctive concentration of Moorish-style civic architecture. Built in 1926, the Opa-locka Company Administration Building is a Moorish Revival landmark at 777 Sharazad Boulevard. Its restoration repaired the façade and updated key building systems while preserving its distinctive forms.

Plan a City Hall visit

Utility billing used to direct customers to the cashier on the fourth floor of City Hall; service is now administered by Miami-Dade County. For County payments, go to Suite 130 at the Municipal Complex. City office hours listed in the FAQ are Monday–Thursday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; some departments maintain separate hours.
