Trash & bulk pickup

Trash days vary by beach district. Bulk waste pickup must be scheduled; the city also lists first-weekend-of-month drop-off events.

Explore this service ↗Building

Permits & inspections

The Building Department supports online Civic Access, permit checklists, inspection scheduling, and some same-day in-person permit services.

Explore this service ↗Public Works

After-hours public works

Public Works lists a 24/7 Control Room for after-hours assistance. Call 305-673-7625; routine department calls are handled during office hours.

GOOD TO KNOW

Check the pickup zone first

Residential trash collection days differ in South Beach, Mid Beach, and North Beach. The resident guide also says bulk pickup must be scheduled. Confirm your block’s calendar before placing items out.

LOCAL DETAIL

There are two useful addresses

Customer Service Center: 1755 Meridian Avenue. City Hall and the Building Department: 1700 Convention Center Drive. Building services also lists a North Beach Annex at 962 Normandy Drive.
