New online pathway · effective Jan 1, 2026

One portal for property and business workflows.

Florida City’s eTRAKiT portal provides 24/7 access to new planning applications, building permits, inspections, business tax licenses and code-compliance services. Existing projects submitted before January 1, 2026 continue through legacy systems.

COUNTY PARTNER

Recycling & disposal support

Miami-Dade County provides curbside recycling to Florida City through an interlocal agreement. Contact County 311 for recycling cart service and missed pickup issues. The County also operates home chemical drop-off centers.
