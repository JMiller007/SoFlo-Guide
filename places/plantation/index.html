Building permits

Accela ACA supports building, right-of-way, and planning permit services. Online applications are available around the clock; account permissions apply to some actions.

GOOD TO KNOW

Not every service is at City Hall

Building Safety is at 401 NW 70th Terrace; Utilities Administration at 500 NW 65th Avenue; Public Works at 750 NW 91st Avenue. Confirm your destination with the city directory.

Quick routing contacts
