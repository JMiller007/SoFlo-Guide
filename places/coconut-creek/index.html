Coconut Creek

Coconut Creek’s daily services follow a greenway-shaped city, but the practical detail residents need first is an address boundary: water and wastewater service changes at Coconut Creek Parkway. This guide separates city utility, Republic Services, permitting, and county help.

A neighborhood-first guide

Find your next step

The first question is often where you live. Coconut Creek does not serve every home with the same water provider; collection and utility questions can go to different desks.

Know your service side

Water service changes at Coconut Creek Parkway.

The City provides water and wastewater north of Coconut Creek Parkway and in southeast Parkland. Homes south of the parkway are served by Margate. Check your bill or address before starting or paying an account.
