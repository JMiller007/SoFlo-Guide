Water, wastewater & your City account

Fort Lauderdale’s Utility Services department operates the local water and wastewater network; Finance handles billing and account service.

START · PAY · CHANGE SERVICE

Utility Billing & Collections

Use the City customer portal to view and pay a water/sewer, sanitation and related utility bill. For new accounts opened on or after March 1, 2026, the City says only property owners can establish accounts under the current ordinance. Existing tenant accounts are unchanged unless service is disconnected. Deposits vary by meter size and service mix.

Utility billing and walk-up support: City Hall, 100 N Andrews Avenue. Phone: 954-828-5150. For a time-sensitive water line issue, call the City’s 24-hour Neighbor Call Center at 954-828-8000.

WATER · WASTEWATER NETWORK

City Utility Services

The City maintains the water-distribution and wastewater-collection network. The utility system also supports treatment services under agreements with Oakland Park, Wilton Manors, Port Everglades and parts of Davie and Tamarac. For a water quality report, interruption, main break or sewer concern, check current notices and contact the City.

GARBAGE · RECYCLING · YARD WASTE

Collection schedule by address

Waste Management collects trash, recycling, yard waste and bulk items for Fort Lauderdale. Bulk collection is monthly on the scheduled service day. City tools and service maps help identify your pickup day, collection guidelines and special programs. For a missed pickup or damaged cart, use Lauderserv or call 954-828-8000.

FLOODING · DRAINS · STREET ISSUE

Lauderserv requests

Use Lauderserv to report a City-maintained street, sidewalk, drain, traffic signal, cart or public-space issue. Include the exact location and a clear description; this helps the City route the request. For immediate hazards or broken utility lines, call the 24-hour center rather than waiting for an online request.

2026 · RESIDENTIAL PERMIT EXEMPTION

Check before assuming a job is permit-free

Florida HB 803 took effect July 1, 2026. Fort Lauderdale explains that some qualifying single-family residential work under $7,500 may be exempt when the City Building Official confirms it qualifies. Commercial, multifamily, flood-zone and several trade-system projects are listed as not exempt. Always verify the specific work and property with Development Services.
