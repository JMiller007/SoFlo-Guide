Public Services is the Town’s practical first stop for physical infrastructure; Town Hall provides the broader contact and department directory.

Water · sewer · stormwater

Town Environmental Services

The Town’s Environmental Services function handles water, sewer, stormwater and drainage issues. For a leak, service interruption, sewer concern or flooding report, call Town Hall and ask for Environmental Services. After-hours sewer emergencies are routed through the Town’s published emergency contacts.

Street · sidewalk · park

Public Services

The Public Services Department maintains Town infrastructure, facilities, streets, sidewalks and parks, and oversees sanitation, recycling, sewer and stormwater programs. For a Town streetlight, right-of-way or public-facility issue, use the Town service request or call the department.

Director’s office: 954-966-4600 ext. 212 · Deputy Director: ext. 600.

Building · zoning · inspections

Building Department

The Town Building Department issues local permit cards and administers the Florida Building Code. Town Hall is open 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; the Building counter is open 7:30 a.m.–4 p.m. and closes for lunch from noon to 1 p.m.

Building counter: 3150 SW 52nd Avenue · 954-966-4600. Check the project checklist and permit portal before bringing plans.

Public records · local decisions

Town Commission & Clerk

Pembroke Park is governed by a five-member Town Commission. The Town website is the source for public meetings, agendas, ordinances, election information and records. Confirm a meeting date and location before attending.

Waste days, plainly

Waste Management is the Town’s contracted collection provider. Bulk is scheduled on specific Wednesdays—not every regular trash day.

Regular household garbage · 2026

Two pickup days each week

The Town’s 2026 notice lists trash collection on Wednesdays and Saturdays. For missed collection, access or account questions, contact Waste Management directly at 954-974-7500.

Oversized household items · 2026

Bulk dates & set-out limits

The published schedule gives six Wednesday bulk dates. Collection accepts furniture, appliances, electronics and similar items too large for the container, up to four cubic yards. Construction material such as drywall, carpet and lumber is excluded.
