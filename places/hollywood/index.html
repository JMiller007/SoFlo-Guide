Start with Hollywood Connect

For non-emergency city concerns, Connect is the central report-and-follow-up path. For billing, waste pickup, active hazards or permits, go straight to the responsible department.

01

Describe the issue and location

Use a street address or clear landmark and explain what happened. A precise location helps the City route a pothole, lighting, drainage or neighborhood concern.

02

Choose the correct service route

Use Connect for general non-emergency concerns. Utility bills and service start/stop go to Utility Billing; missed garbage or recycling goes to Environmental Services/Waste Pro; construction goes through Building Permits.

The City’s departments handle different steps. Here’s where to begin and what to confirm.

Water · sewer · stormwater

Confirm your address is City-served

Hollywood asks customers to confirm the property is served by its Department of Public Utilities before applying. Utility Billing is at 2600 Hollywood Boulevard, Room 103. The City lists a deposit for new accounts; call 954-921-3938 for application requirements, account changes, bills or reconnection help.

Trash · recycling · bulk

Check your collection map

Waste Pro handles garbage collection twice each week. Single-family homes and duplexes should use the City-issued gray garbage cart. Hollywood’s address map shows collection days for garbage, recycling, yard waste and bulk waste; service rules differ by property type. Waste Pro: 954-967-4200; cart repair: Environmental Services at 954-967-4526.

Building · renovations · inspections

Choose the right permit portal

For new permit applications, Hollywood moved to Accela Citizen Access effective August 4, 2026. The City says the old BCLA portal is for existing BCLA permits only. Accela supports new applications, document uploads, permit management and record search. Building permits: 954-921-3335; inspections: 954-921-3250.

Meetings, records and contacts
