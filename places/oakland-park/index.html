A quicker way to city services

Pick the task. Then call the right desk.

Oakland Park’s municipal building concentrates many resident-facing teams at 1100 Park Lane East. The department floor and counter hours differ, so use the detail below before making a trip.

01 · Water + utility bill954-630-4280

Account setup, billing questions, and service restoration. Email ubs@oaklandparkfl.gov. Utility counter: weekdays, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

After-hours utility emergency: 954-707-5184

During regular business hours, utility emergency line: 954-630-4430. The city purchases its water from Fort Lauderdale.

02 · Trash pickup954-630-4452

Ask about your residential garbage collection day or a missed service. The city collects on weekday holidays except Christmas Day.

Use the city’s current bulk-trash guide for set-out rules and collection information.

03 · Permit + inspection954-630-4350

Building and Permitting is at 1100 Park Lane East, first floor. Counter hours: weekdays, 7:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Online inspection requests must be submitted by 3 p.m. on the business day before the requested inspection.
