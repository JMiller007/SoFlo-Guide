Water · bill · service account

Water Division

West Miami's Water Division is the city contact for water bills, water service and water-quality information. For payment, use the City portal, pay by check in person or by mail, or call the automated attendant. City Hall Water and Sanitation can help by phone at 305-266-1222.

Online portal accepts cards; in-person payment is listed as check or card; mailed payments are checks only. The posted bill page lists an automated card line at 888-422-3250.

Building · land use · inspections

Building Department

The City issues building, electrical, mechanical, roofing, window/door and air-conditioning permits. The department checks compliance with City, County and Florida Building Code. Some projects need both a City permit and outside agency approvals.

901 SW 62 Avenue · weekdays, 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m. · 305-266-4214. Inspection requests are handled during the posted office window.

TRASH · RECYCLING

Sanitation Division

Use the City Sanitation Division for residential curbside collection, service rates, fees and schedules. Miami-Dade also provides curbside recycling under an interlocal agreement. Exact pickup days depend on the service route and address.

WATER · SEWER HANDOFF

City and County roles

Start with West Miami Water Division for your local water account. Regional wastewater service involves Miami-Dade WASD, while the City operates facilities on its side of the County connection. For a sewer emergency or billing issue, call the responsible utility before dispatching a contractor.
