CITY RESPONSE · 24 HOURS

Report a problem through 311

Use 311 for City service requests such as missed trash, abandoned vehicles, damaged infrastructure, code issues and neighborhood concerns. Call 311 within Miami-Dade or 305-468-5900, or use the City’s Lauderserv-style Miami service request tools and track the request.

TRASH · RECYCLING · BULK

City Solid Waste schedule

City residential garbage is collected twice weekly. Residential bulky trash is collected once a week, with address-based schedules and set-out guidance. Look up your own pickup calendar, put bins out 12–24 hours before the morning pickup, and keep bulky items separate from household carts.

CONSTRUCTION · ZONING

Apply or manage a permit in iBuild

iBuild supports City of Miami permit applications, contractor management, plan changes, fee payments and inspection scheduling. Some projects also need County approvals, zoning review, or right-of-way permits. Confirm jurisdiction first; City of Miami permits are separate from Miami-Dade County permitting.

City Hall & regional handoffs

City Hall is on the waterfront at Dinner Key. Water and sewer are a County service, with a City stormwater assessment billed through the County utility account.

MEETINGS · RECORDS · CITY CLERK

Go to City Hall at Dinner Key

The City Clerk’s Office and Commission Chambers are at 3500 Pan American Drive. Clerk public records are available Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. City Commission meetings are held at City Hall; check the current meeting calendar, agenda and participation instructions.

WATER · SEWER · REGIONAL

Miami-Dade WASD

Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer provides drinking water and wastewater services to many Miami addresses. Confirm provider for the exact address. WASD customer care is 305-665-7477; use the County’s account portal for start/stop service, bills, payment and service requests.

FLOODING · DRAINAGE · RESILIENCE

City stormwater utility

The City’s stormwater master plan funds upgrades to reduce flooding and protect public infrastructure. The $7 monthly residential stormwater fee effective February 2026 appears on the Miami-Dade WASD bill for properties with a County account. For a clogged City drain or street flooding, report the location to City 311.

NEIGHBORHOOD INVESTMENT

NET service centers

Neighborhood Enhancement Teams connect City departments with residents and businesses in local areas. For community events, neighborhood concerns and local resources, start with the NET team serving your address or City 311.
