LIGHTHOUSE POINT · A WATERFRONT CITY

Small city.
Easy ways around.

Lighthouse Point’s civic center sits a few blocks from the water, while a free, wheelchair-accessible community shuttle connects everyday destinations. Here’s the practical map: City Hall for permits and records, Public Works for neighborhood service, and the shuttle for a ride across town.

A neighborhood shuttle is part of the city service map.

The Lighthouse Point Community Shuttle is wheelchair accessible and does not require a fare. Check the City’s shuttle page for current route, stop locations and hours. For mobility or stop questions, call City Hall before traveling.

Garbage & bulk

Household garbage and bulk trash are collected twice each week, with holiday service except Christmas Day. Keep bulk separate and on your property beside the curb. Set-out may begin after 3 p.m. the day before; retrieve containers by 9 p.m. on collection day.

Collection rules ↗
02

Business tax & permits

Before opening a business, contact Planning and Zoning to confirm the use is permitted. The local business tax application goes through Clerk review and must then be cleared by Building/Zoning, Fire, Code Enforcement and Police.

Local business tax process ↗
03

Building & zoning

The City’s public permit portal supports permit and business-tax-license lookup. Building & Zoning questions go to 954-943-6509; inspection line: 954-784-3449.

City records & meetings

The City Clerk is the initial information point for public records and maintains Commission agendas, meeting minutes, ordinances, resolutions, bids and election information.
