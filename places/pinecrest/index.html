At the Village

Building and Planning is where permits, zoning and local business approvals begin. Public records can be inspected at Village Hall on weekdays.

Building & development

Pinecrest issues its own permit number first; applicants may then need to submit it with Miami-Dade County applications. Use the Village eTRAKiT portal for permits, inspections and project status. Separate permits may apply to grading, demolition, electrical, mechanical and plumbing work.

Tree removal or relocation generally needs a Village permit before the work begins. For a business, confirm zoning and use approval before signing a lease; the Village and County both issue business tax receipts.

Public records & open government

Village records are available for inspection at 12645 Pinecrest Parkway, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. A written request is not required to inspect public records. Copy and special-service charges may apply for reproduction or extensive staff time.

The Village Clerk is the records custodian. Call 305-234-2121 for routing or submit a records request through the Village Clerk’s page.

Neighborhood issue or code concern

Use MyPinecrest311 to report an issue. Include the location so staff can route the request. Code Compliance handles local property and zoning concerns; anonymous complaints may not be investigated under Florida law.

Parks, gardens & programs

Pinecrest Parks & Recreation manages Village parks, facilities, programs and reservations. Create a Community Pass account to register for programs or reserve a park amenity, field or court. Check individual facility hours and notices before visiting.

On-demand electric rides
$3.50

Village Freebee rides cost $3.50 per ride, plus $1 for each extra passenger. Service connects destinations around Pinecrest with the South Dade Transitway and Metrorail station. The Village lists Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–7 p.m. and Saturday 10 a.m.–10 p.m. Request through the Ride Freebee app; ADA/dispatch line 855-918-3733.

Miami-Dade County · everyday services

Waste, water & regional help

County Solid Waste handles garbage, recycling and bulky pickup. Use the County address lookup or call 311 for a missed pickup or to schedule bulk collection. Miami-Dade Water & Sewer handles new water/sewer service and utility emergencies; Pinecrest lists 305-665-7477 for new service. Florida City Gas serves the area.
