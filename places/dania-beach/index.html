The curbside and neighborhood rhythm

Weekly collection. Monthly bulk.

Waste Management handles service under the City’s Public Services Department. Collection days and bulk zones vary by address, so start with the current City schedule map.

GarbageTwice each week

City guidance says household garbage is collected twice weekly. Use the schedule map to find your days.

RecyclingOnce each week

Use the assigned recycling day and city-issued cart; check current accepted-material rules.

Bulk wasteMonthly by zone

Six numbered zones each have a monthly route day. The Spring 2025 city newsletter limits residential bulk to six cubic yards.

SET-OUT RULEPlace carts by 7 a.m. on collection day. The City’s spring 2025 guidance says bulk should not be placed curbside more than 48 hours before pickup.
COLLECTION QUESTIONSContact Waste Management at 954-974-7500. For city public-service or utility questions, call the relevant Dania Beach department.
