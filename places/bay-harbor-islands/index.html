NEW RESIDENT · MOVE · CLOSE ACCOUNT

Town utility billing

New utility accounts are established through Town Hall. The utility account includes water, sewer, stormwater and solid waste. The Town requires deposits; amounts depend on the service and account. Have the property address, move-in date and account-holder documentation ready.

Utility billing: 305-866-6241. Town Hall: 9665 Bay Harbor Terrace.

WATER · REGIONAL SUPPLY

Who supplies the drinking water?

The Town owns and operates its local water system but purchases drinking water from Miami-Dade WASD as a wholesale customer. The Town distributes it to local properties. For a Town account or local line issue, begin with Town Utilities; published Town guidance sends water and sewer emergencies to Police dispatch for routing.

EMERGENCY · SEWER · WATER

Service interruption

For a water or sewer emergency, the Town’s published utility page directs residents to Police at 305-866-6242 so staff can route the call. For account balances, move service or deposits, call Town Hall at 305-866-6241 during business hours.

TRASH · RECYCLING · SET-OUT

Solid waste and recycling

The Town provides solid waste and recycling information and bills solid-waste service through its utility system. The Town has set-out rules for trash, yard waste, recycling and bulky materials; don't leave bags or bulky items at the curb outside collection requirements, as a fee may apply.

Town Hall for everything else

Building permits, town records and community services all start with the local government office on Bay Harbor Terrace.

CONSTRUCTION · ZONING · CODE

Building & Zoning

Most construction work requires a Town building permit. The Town uses the Citizenserve online portal for construction permits and other applications. Start with the property address and scope of work; ask Building staff about zoning, plan review and any County approvals needed for your project.

Town infrastructure

Public Works maintains Town-owned water and sewer utilities and supports streets, drainage and public infrastructure. Describe the location and issue clearly when contacting Town Hall; it helps staff route work to the right crew.
