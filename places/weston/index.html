A useful place to begin

Weston provides an address-driven GIS guide for local services. Its waste calendar is also address-specific, with residential bulk pickups four times a year in January, April, July, and October.

Explore this service ↗Bulk waste

Quarterly bulk pickup

Residential bulk collection is scheduled in January, April, July, and October. Your specific week and day depend on your address; contractor debris and electronics are excluded.

Explore this service ↗Resident help

Contact Public Works

For collection issues, Weston lists Republic Services at 954-583-1830 and solidwaste@westonfl.org. City Hall is at 17200 Royal Palm Blvd.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bulk pickup is quarterly, not on demand

Weston residents receive four scheduled bulk collections each year. The neighborhood map sets the exact collection day; special pickup may be available for a fee. Check the excluded-items list before placing anything out.

LOCAL DETAIL

The address map goes beyond waste

My City Services can also help locate zoning, permit, code, flood, school, voting, park, public-safety, and transportation information for a selected address.
