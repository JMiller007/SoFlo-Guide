Parkland’s landscape feels quiet and spacious; its service map is just as important. The city itself does not provide every utility or safety service. Follow this guide to the right provider for your address, project or pickup.

Four parts of the Parkland service map

Parkland uses a mix of city departments, regional partners and utility providers. Ask Parkland is the city’s routing page for the common cases below.

01 · Water, sewer, powerOutside utilities

Utility services are managed by outside providers. Use the city’s provider lookup for water/sewer; FPL handles electricity.
