GOOD TO KNOW

City Hall and permit counters are in different buildings

Customer Service and Water Billing are at City Hall, 7525 NW 88th Avenue, Room 102. The Building Department is at 6011 Nob Hill Road. Confirm the right location before visiting.

LOCAL DETAIL

Two numbers for utility issues

For bill payments and utility accounts, call Customer Service at 954-597-3590. The city’s contact directory lists 954-597-3750 for water service issues such as leaks and breaks, including after-hours routing.
