Residential service

Garbage & bulk collection

Household garbage is collected twice weekly and bulk weekly. Use brown city carts, place them at the curb by 7 a.m., and follow the city’s bulk set-out limits.

GOOD TO KNOW

Curbside recycling is suspended

The city currently directs residents to recycling drop-off options. Confirm the latest status and available locations on the official Solid Waste page before transporting materials.

LOCAL DETAIL

Bulk pickup has set-out limits

The city’s residential service page says bulk items may be set out no more than 24 hours before collection and are limited to 7 cubic yards. Special materials may have separate handling rules.
