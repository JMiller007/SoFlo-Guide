WILTON MANORS · THE ISLAND CITY

City services,
one island desk.

In Wilton Manors, City Hall’s Utility Billing team is the front door for water, sewer, garbage, recycling and stormwater charges. This guide follows the island’s actual service pattern: pay and report through the City, check your collection zone, and take permits online.

Collection has a neighborhood rhythm

Know your side of the island.

Single-family homes, townhomes and buildings up to six units have residential service. The city divides collection into Northwest and Southeast zones; each zone’s weekly days differ.

Garbage × 2Twice per week. Your zone determines the collection days.
Recycling × 1Combined with one of your garbage days: Monday or Tuesday.
Bulk + HHW × 1Monthly service. Open the current calendar and zone map before set-out.
City-issued carts only: the City warns that other cities’ or private carts left at the curb may not be serviced. For a missed pickup, call Utility Billing at 954-390-2145 or the after-hours line 954-390-2190 and leave a message.

01 · Household account

One bill, several city services

Utility Billing consolidates water and sewer usage with garbage, recycling, stormwater and household hazardous-waste charges. The Finance Division can explain line items, fees, start/stop service and payment channels.

Understand utility billing ↗

Water or sewer emergency? During regular business hours call the City. After-hours emergency routing is 954-390-2150 during designated dispatch hours; at other times use the city’s phone menu, option 8. Routine billing does not go through emergency dispatch.

02 · Build & improve

Permits are submitted electronically

Community Development Services handles building permits, inspections, zoning and neighborhood planning. Permit submissions are electronic; its office is on City Hall’s second floor and is open Monday–Thursday, 7 a.m.–6 p.m.

Community Development Services ↗

For a permit record or other public document, use the City’s online public-record request portal rather than relying on a general inbox.
