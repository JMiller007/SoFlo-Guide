SOUTHWEST RANCHES · PRESERVE OUR RURAL LIFESTYLE

Room to roam.
A different kind of town desk.

Southwest Ranches is a low-density, agricultural town on Broward’s western edge. Permits, drainage and collection all work through address-specific processes and zones. The Town’s online tools are especially useful when your property sits on a boundary street or requires county approvals.

PROPERTY · BUILD

Permit through CAP online

CAP Government staff work from Town Hall, but building permit applications and plans are submitted electronically. Forms include the Broward County Uniform Building Permit Application and project-specific packages. Several projects need Town or County zoning/engineering approvals first.

LAND · WATER

Drainage, roads & review

Public Works and Engineering handle infrastructure, drainage, capital projects and coordination of development approvals. Use the Town’s Fill Permit Log, Pothole Log and Development Permits tools to understand active work and review requirements.

Ask the Town Clerk

The Clerk maintains official Town Council and board records, including minutes, ordinances, resolutions and contracts. Requests can be submitted through the public-record portal or by contacting the Clerk.

2026 service map

Collection follows the land, by zone.

Waste Management provides Town residential service. The Town publishes separate maps for garbage, recycling and bulk. Boundary streets follow the zone assigned to the street name, so use the official map before relying on a weekday alone.

GarbageTwo collection days each week; areas follow Monday/Thursday, Tuesday/Friday, or Wednesday/Saturday routes.
RecyclingWeekly route varies by area; the schedule shows Wednesday, Thursday or Friday service.
BulkMonthly service dates are assigned to 12 numbered zones. 2026 calendar lists each zone’s dates.

For service questions, call Waste Management at 954-974-7500. Keep carts clear of parked cars, mailboxes, trees, utility poles and fences; follow the Town’s full curbside guide for item limits and placement.

Ranch resident essentials

Common questions, direct routes

Town Hall can route you to the right person at 954-434-0008. For emergencies, call 911.

Start a building permit

Building applications are submitted through CAP’s online portal. Staff hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., with lunch closure noon–1 p.m. Call 954-434-0008, option 1.
