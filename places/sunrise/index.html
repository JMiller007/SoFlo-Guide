A useful place to begin

Your utility bill may include water, sewer, stormwater, garbage, and recycling. Billing and service requests go through Public Service; infrastructure emergencies use the after-hours utility line.

SoFlo Guide summarizes public information to help you find the right service. Each link below points to the government source for current requirements.

Utility emergency contacts

For after-hours utility emergencies, call 954-888-6087 or 954-764-4357. Routine billing questions go to Public Service at 954-746-3232.

Explore this service ↗Building Division

Building permits & inspections

Building Division handles permits and inspections at 10770 W. Oakland Park Blvd. Published hours are Mon–Thu 8 a.m.–5 p.m. and Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

GOOD TO KNOW

Utility emergency after hours

For urgent utility issues outside normal business hours, Sunrise lists 954-888-6087 and 954-764-4357. For routine billing or service questions, contact Public Service during its weekday business hours.

LOCAL DETAIL

Three Public Service counters

Besides City Hall at 10770 W. Oakland Park Blvd., the city lists counters at Village Civic Center (6800 Sunset Strip) and New River Civic Center (60 Weston Rd.). Check each location’s hours before visiting.
